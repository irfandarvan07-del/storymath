## Project summary

StoryMath is an intelligent, bilingual mathematics platform that decouples underlying mathematical structures from surface story narratives, using 5-step diagnostic scaffolding, interactive visual models, and a Gemini AI-powered teacher copilot to pinpoint and remediate foundational learning bottlenecks for Class 5–7 students across Karnataka.

**1. What made you choose this problem?**

According to ASER 2024 and NCERT PARAKH reports, over 80% of Class 5 students in rural Karnataka government schools struggle with basic division word problems, and only 53% of Class 6 students can apply arithmetic to daily life scenarios. Traditional grading simply marks an answer wrong, masking whether the student stumbled on language comprehension, operation choice, visual representation, or arithmetic calculation. We built StoryMath to isolate the exact cognitive breakpoint across 5 structured steps while embedding problems in culturally vibrant Karnataka contexts like Channapatna toys, ISRO missions, Lalbagh, and Bengaluru Metro.

**2. Who is it for, and how would they use it in a normal week?**

StoryMath is built for Class 5–7 students and mathematics educators in Karnataka government and state schools. In a normal week, students log into their verified school profile and assigned class code (e.g. CLASS-4C) to solve scaffolded word problems in English or Kannada, building bar models and selecting operations before computing answers and completing transfer problems. Teachers log into the single Global Teacher Portal to inspect cohort-wide failure heatmaps, diagnose stuck student clusters, and review actionable 40-minute remediation lesson plans generated by the Gemini AI Pedagogical Copilot.

**3. If you had another month, what would you build next?**

With another month, we would introduce full Kannada audio narration with speech recognition for first-generation learners who possess strong verbal mathematical intuition but struggle with written text. We would also implement lightweight WhatsApp and SMS digest notifications for rural teachers, allowing them to receive 1-page printable remedial activity sheets without requiring high-speed broadband. Finally, we would link our diagnostic failure taxonomy directly with the Karnataka State Board syllabus and DIKSHA / Samagra Shiksha Karnataka Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs).
