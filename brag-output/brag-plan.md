# Brag Plan: StoryMath

## What is this app?
StoryMath is an intelligent, bilingual mathematics platform for Classes 5–7 that decouples underlying mathematical structures from surface stories, using a 5-step diagnostic scaffold, interactive visual bar models, and a Gemini AI teacher copilot to pinpoint and remediate learning bottlenecks across Karnataka.

## The angle
Traditional testing marks a word problem wrong with a binary 0 or 1. It never tells the teacher whether the student stumbled on language comprehension, distinguishing given vs asked facts, visual modeling, operation choice, or arithmetic calculation. Furthermore, students treat every new story as a new problem because they memorize surface keywords. StoryMath holds the schema invariant while changing the story across vibrant Karnataka contexts (Channapatna toys, Mysuru Pak, Bengaluru Metro, ISRO).
